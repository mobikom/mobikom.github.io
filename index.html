Institutional Principles & Resource Economics

  • Zero Persistent Tracking: The platform writes nothing to client disk storage — operating without cookies, tracking pixels, or dynamic CRM telemetry.
  • Volatile RAM Execution: Rendered states exist strictly in transient memory pages, leaving zero forensic trace upon closing the session.
  • Cryptographic Trust (DNSSEC): End-to-end domain authenticity anchored cryptographically at the national registry level.
  • Direct Operational Correspondence: Corporate inquiries and verified banking wire transfers (Postbank IBAN) are managed without intermediary data brokers.
