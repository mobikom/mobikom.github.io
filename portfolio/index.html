TECHNICAL PASSPORT • REGIONAL MEDIA

dobrichnews.com — Regional Daily News Portal

Official URL: https://www.dobrichnews.com →

Daily digital newspaper delivering verified public-interest journalism, municipal investigative reporting, and real-time civic alerts across Dobrich, Balchik, Kavarna, Shabla, General Toshevo, Tervel, and Northeastern Bulgaria.

  • Founding Chronicle: 2011 founding milestone within the regional media consolidation.
  • Editorial Charter: Independent, non-partisan journalism protecting civic accountability, environmental compliance, and regional transparency.
  • Network Syndication: Real-time cross-syndicated headline feed integrated into the primary Mobikom hub.
TECHNICAL PASSPORT • CULTURE & AGRARIAN REVIEW

dobruja.com — Cultural & Agrarian Journal of Dobruja

Official URL: https://www.dobruja.com →

Specialized regional journal preserving Dobruja historical memory, agrarian traditions, folklore ethnography, and agricultural sector analysis across the Danube basin and Black Sea coastal region.

  • Historical Lineage: Initial publishing operations begun in 2007; formally institutionalized in 2011.
  • Thematic Scope: Agribusiness, regional grain market evaluation, ethnography, and Dobruja heritage.
  • Archival Role: Preserves digitized monographs and agricultural historical chronicles.
TECHNICAL PASSPORT • CAREER CLASSIFIEDS BOARD

bestjobs.bg — Career & Direct Employment Board

Official URL: https://bestjobs.bg →

National direct job advertising board connecting employers and career candidates across Bulgaria without recruiter fees or middleman commissions.

  • Institutional Provenance: Established national brand originating from the regional classifieds network of Neogen Group in Southeastern Europe, consolidated under Mobikom Bulgaria on August 15, 2013.
  • Operational Standards: Standardized salary brackets in Euro (€), direct contact channels with employers, and zero applicant fees.
  • Architecture: 100% zero-cookie architecture, complete applicant privacy, and sub-150ms execution speed.
TECHNICAL PASSPORT • DIGITAL RESEARCH ARCHIVE

Sea Stars — Pomorie Lake Lye & Mineral Salts Monograph

Monograph Link: Read the complete Pomorie Lake Lye Monograph →

Digital scientific archive and catalog case study documenting the therapeutic, balneological, and biochemical properties of authentic Black Sea mineral salts and Pomorie lake lye, based on applied research by the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS).

  • Scientific Value: Trace mineral analysis (high magnesium, calcium, and iodine concentration) applied to therapeutic and spa tourism.
  • Ecosystem Role: Part of the network's destination stewardship along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast.
