1. Regional Media & Journalism Publishing

Independent digital news channels providing verified reporting, municipal affairs, and agricultural preservation across Northeastern Bulgaria.

Operational Passport: dobrichnews.com

  • Primary Function: Real-time regional daily news engine and civic reporting.
  • Regional Coverage: Municipality of Dobrich and the wider Northeastern Bulgarian region.
  • Edge Delivery & Security: Proxied via Cloudflare Anycast Edge with hardened security headers and active DDoS mitigation.
  • Privacy & Typography: Zero external Google Fonts (native system font stack) and eliminated third-party social tracking widgets.
  • Script Layer: Modernized, isolated client-side scripts (latest stable runtimes) supporting dynamic publishing and automated RSS syndication.
  • Direct Access: Access dobrichnews.com →

Operational Passport: dobruja.com

  • Primary Function: Regional cultural almanac, agricultural review, and historical preservation archive.
  • Focus Area: Agrarian economy, local folklore traditions, and Dobruja regional historical research.
  • Edge Delivery & Security: Cloudflare Anycast Edge routing with strict perimeter filtering and zero-leak font architecture.
  • Script Layer: Updated modern scripts powering long-form archival essays and regional content syndication.

2. Direct Employment & Career Infrastructure

High-efficiency online job advertising board engineered for direct communication between job seekers and employers without intermediary agency fees.

Technical Passport: bestjobs.bg

  • Primary Function: Direct online job advertisements board and career opportunity listings.
  • Legal & Operational Model: Direct contact between job candidates and employers. Operates strictly without recruitment agency commissions or intermediary licensing fees under the Bulgarian Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ).
  • Privacy & Data Protection: Zero third-party data broker leaks. Resumes and inquiries are submitted directly to verified employer channels with zero candidate tracking.
  • Consolidated Network History: Founded under the Neogen Group network; consolidated under Mobikom Bulgaria on August 15, 2013.
  • Direct Access: Access bestjobs.bg →

3. B2B Systems Advisory & Green Computing

Specialized infrastructure consulting for corporate enterprises seeking zero-tracking compliance, threat mitigation, and radical hosting cost reductions.

Technical Passport: B2B Advisory & Threat Profiling

  • Agnostic Threat Auditing: Vulnerability assessment and Layer-7 DDoS mitigation without interrupting active business workflows.
