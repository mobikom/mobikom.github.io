About Mobikom

Engineering lightweight digital media, civic journalism, and static web resilience in Bulgaria since 2011.

1. Mission & Infrastructure Philosophy

Mobikom operates as an independent technical and digital media network headquartered in Bulgaria. In an era dominated by bloated web frameworks, aggressive cross-site tracking, and centralized walled gardens, Mobikom implements a privacy-first, deterministic architecture: zero behavioral cookies, clean semantic HTML5, and low-power static delivery.

The network's primary directive is to ensure that regional news reporting, career discovery boards, and cultural archives remain accessible on any device, over any connection speed, with minimal energy consumption.

2. Analog Cellular Beginnings (1992)

The technological lineage of the Mobikom name in Bulgaria traces back to 1992 with the rollout of the nation’s first commercial cellular network using Nordic Mobile Telephone (NMT-450) standards. Operating at 450 MHz, these analog "Mobifons" delivered long-range wireless connectivity across Bulgaria’s complex mountainous terrain.

While analog modulation eventually gave way to modern digital standards, the core engineering principle established during that era—maximizing signal efficiency and geographic reach under strict hardware constraints—remains central to our current web operations.

3. Pagers & Mobika Card Payphones

Throughout the mid-to-late 1990s, the national infrastructure expanded into widespread radio paging networks (beepers) and the iconic "Mobika" chip-card payphone terminals installed across Bulgarian municipalities. These systems established the foundational public expectation for reliable, distributed everyday communications.

Today, Mobikom preserves this communications heritage not as nostalgia, but as an architectural benchmark: public communications must remain durable, lightweight, and operable without unnecessary barriers to entry.

4. 2011 Operations Founding & The Static Web

In 2011, Mobikom was reconstituted into an independent digital media operations desk. Departing from heavy commercial CMS stacks, the network adopted the principles of open syndication (RSS/Atom), decentralized protocols (OStatus), and the 512-Byte Web Principle.

Today, the network actively directs key Bulgarian digital properties:

  • dobrichnews.com: Real-time verified regional journalism serving Dobrich and North-East Bulgaria.
  • dobruja.com: Cultural preservation, agricultural records, and community history of the Dobruja region.
  • bestjobs.bg: High-efficiency online employment discovery and direct career postings.
  • Digital Archives & Monographs: Independent research platforms including the Black Sea mineral chemistry and Pomorie lye initiative.
