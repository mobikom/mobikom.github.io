About Mobikom: History & Institutional Network

From Wireless Cellular Pioneer to Autonomous Linux Infrastructure, Regional Media Networks & High-Availability Web Standards.

Documenting the technological journey of the mobikom.bg namespace—from the foundational Nordic Mobile Telephony (NMT-450) analog cellular network in 1992, through autonomous Linux server clusters (Web, Mail, FTP, Databases, Red5 Media Streaming), Dobruja Media Group's internet television broadcasts from Cannes and Johannesburg, to the domain's reconstitution in 2011 into an independent institutional digital network under General Manager Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA.

Public Archival & Educational Notice

Historical telecommunications trademarks (RTC Mobikom, Mobifons, Paging systems, Mobika), international employment network records (Neogen Group, BestJobs International), and multimedia broadcasting archives (Dobruja Media Group, Red5 streaming nodes, Cannes broadcasts, Johannesburg Mandela residence coverage) are documented on this portal strictly for educational, cultural, and archival reference. The domain mobikom.bg was acquired in 2011 and organized by Mobikom Bulgaria for independent digital operations.

1. 1992 – 2005: The NMT-450 Cellular Pioneer

In December 1992, Radio Telecommunication Company (RTC) Mobikom was incorporated as a landmark international joint venture between British telecommunications giant Cable & Wireless (49%), the state-owned Bulgarian Telecommunications Company (BTC / БТК, 39%), and Radioelectronic Systems (12%). It became Bulgaria's first commercial mobile network operator.

  • The NMT-450i Standard: Operating on the 450 MHz analog frequency band, Mobikom deployed base stations with massive propagation radii exceeding 30 to 40 kilometers, achieving over 95% territorial coverage across Bulgarian highways, mountainous regions, and the Black Sea coastline.
  • Mobifons & Dialing Codes: Reaching over 196,000 active subscribers, the network originally utilized the national dial code 0799 with 5-digit subscriber numbers, transitioning to the iconic 048 dialing prefix with 6-digit subscriber numbering.

2. Handsets, Paging Systems & Collectible Mobika Cards

Analog NMT-450 handsets were hard-programmed in Mobikom service centers by electronic serial number (ESN). Iconic models included the legendary Benefon Sigma / Sigma Gold (the 1994 World Cup sensation), the indestructible Benefon Dragon, Benefon Delta & Spica, and the Nokia 440, 450, 540, 640, and 650 series.

Concurrently, Mobikom operated Bulgaria's primary alphanumeric paging network (Motorola Advisor and Motorola Bravo beepers) and thousands of card-operated street payphone booths under the "Mobika" brand (25, 50, 100, 200, and 400 impulse cards), giving rise to the nationwide telecartophily (фонокартия) phonecard collecting movement.

3. 2007 – 2012: The Shift to Software Protocols & Federated Networks

Following the closure of the analog NMT-450 network on August 5, 2008, the telecommunications paradigm shifted from physical spectrum hardware to distributed software, open web protocols, and decentralized services. The domain mobikom.bg was acquired in 2011 and organized by Mobikom Bulgaria under the leadership of Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA specifically to expand next-generation open technologies:

  • 2007 – The moutarde.org Community: Early French-oriented microblogging pioneers and open-web enthusiasts explored real-time message exchange outside corporate walled gardens.
  • Acquisition of mobikom.bg & OStatus: The architecture integrated the pioneering work of Evan Prodromou (founder of StatusNet, identi.ca, and lead author of the OStatus standard), deploying mobikom.bg as an open microblogging node with real-time status streams.
  • pump.io & Diaspora* Federated Ecosystem: The infrastructure benchmarked Evan Prodromou's next-generation pump.io protocol (the architectural blueprint for W3C ActivityPub) alongside cross-network bridges connecting the decentralized Diaspora* social network and early precursors to the modern Fediverse.

4. 2011 – 2015: Server Infrastructure, Internet TV & Destination Media

Organized under Mobikom Bulgaria, this era marked the deployment of comprehensive hardware and software server infrastructure supporting the transition of Dobruja Media Group from traditional print publishing to interactive web media, live streaming, and broadcast partnerships:

  • Autonomous Linux Server Infrastructure: Deployed proprietary bare-metal and virtualized Linux server clusters (Debian, Ubuntu, CentOS, RedHat) running high-performance web servers (Nginx, Apache), enterprise mail servers (Postfix, Dovecot), secure FTP daemons (ProFTPD), and relational database engines (MySQL, MariaDB).
  • Red5 Media Streaming: Engineered real-time RTMP, RTSP, and HLS streaming pipelines utilizing the open-source Red5 Media Server and FFmpeg transcoding, supporting automated scheduling, documentary broadcasts, and live camera streams.
  • Black Sea Tourism & Destination Media: Extensive resort media coverage and content partnerships promoting premier Bulgarian Black Sea destinations, including Albena Resort and St. St. Constantine & Helena, in active synergy with tourism journalist Ruslan Nikolov.
  • Sports Journalism & Digital Audiobooks: Launch of sport.dobruja.com (August 1, 2011) alongside the literary audio streaming platform audiobooks.dobruja.com.
  • International Channels & Cannes Cinema Broadcasts: Streamed film releases from Cannes, France, alongside specialized endpoints like africa.dobruja.com (broadcasting nature channels and historical coverage from Johannesburg including visits to the residence of Nobel Peace Prize laureate Nelson Mandela).
  • Broadcaster Syndication & Institutional Partnerships: Under the editorial guidance of Honorary Editor-in-Chief Violeta Varbanova, the media group established content sharing and news cooperation with major television networks: bTV, Nova News, TV7, Bulgarian National Television (BNT), Deutsche Welle (DW), Associated Press (AP), CNN, leading national news agencies, and London-based newspapers serving the UK Bulgarian expatriate community.

5. The Evolution of bestjobs.bg • Direct Recruitment Classifieds

The recruitment and employment classifieds arm of our network holds deep roots in the Eastern European digital job market:

  • Origin within the Neogen Group: bestjobs.bg was originally founded as part of the regional digital employment network built by the Neogen Group (founded by entrepreneur Călin Fusu), which pioneered online job matching across Eastern Europe alongside sister platforms in Romania, Serbia, and Moldova.
  • Regional Demand via jobs.dobruja.com: Under Dobruja Media Group, the local classifieds platform demonstrated an acute demand for direct, unencumbered employment listings in Bulgaria.
  • Consolidation & Technical Independence (August 15, 2013 – Present): On August 15, 2013, the operations and infrastructure of bestjobs.bg consolidated under Mobikom Bulgaria. Today, bestjobs.bg operates as a direct online job ads board connecting employers and candidates with direct contact and zero intermediary recruiter fees.

6. Present Day: High-Availability Institutional Network

Today, Mobikom functions as an umbrella institutional gateway managing high-availability digital publishing engines, direct employment boards, and applied computer science research:

  • bestjobs.bg: Direct online job ads board connecting employers and candidates across Bulgaria with direct contact and zero intermediary recruiter fees.
  • dobrichnews.com: Real-time regional daily news engine delivering verified journalism, civic reporting, and municipal affairs to North-East Bulgaria.
  • dobruja.com: Regional cultural preservation journal, economic review, and community archive for the Dobruja region.
  • Sea Stars Monograph: Curated digital archive and catalog case study on Pomorie therapeutic lake lye and Black Sea mineral salts.
