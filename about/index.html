INSTITUTIONAL CHRONICLE • 1992 – 2026

Telecommunications Heritage & Network History

Chronicle of the paradigm shift from Bulgaria's first analog cellular network to decentralized software protocols, regional media sovereignty, and 512-byte web architecture.

1. 1992 – 2008: The Analog Cellular Pioneer (RTC Mobikom)

In 1992, Bulgaria's first mobile telecommunications enterprise was established — Radio Telecommunication Company (RTC) Mobikom, formed as a joint venture between British telecommunications carrier Cable & Wireless, Bulgarian Telecommunications Company (BTC), and RELAX.

The operator deployed the nation's first cellular infrastructure adhering to the Nordic Mobile Telephone standard on 450 MHz (NMT-450), introducing iconic analog Mobifon terminals by Benefon and Nokia. The network delivered resilient coverage across remote rural territories a decade before widespread GSM adoption. On August 5, 2008, analog NMT-450 broadcasts in Bulgaria were permanently discontinued, closing the hardware cellular era.

2. Motorola Paging Systems & Mobika Phonecard Infrastructure

Concurrently with analog cellular operations, Mobikom engineered a nationwide paging network utilizing Motorola Advisor terminals, providing real-time text dispatch for emergency services, energy grids, and public institutions.

During the late 1990s, the consortium deployed an extensive public payphone network powered by Mobika smart chip phonecards, representing a foundational milestone in Bulgarian urban connectivity.

3. 2007 – 2012: The Shift to Software Protocols & Federated Microblogging

Following the decommission of analog transmitters in 2008, telecommunications shifted from physical spectrum hardware to distributed software protocols. Beginning in 2007, early open-web foundations were established via the French-language community moutarde.org, exploring real-time message exchange outside corporate walled gardens.

In 2011, the institutional domain mobikom.bg was consolidated by Mobikom Bulgaria specifically to operate as an open federated microblogging node. The architecture integrated the open OStatus standard (StatusNet / identi.ca) and the pump.io protocol — the technical blueprint later standardized by the W3C as ActivityPub. The infrastructure maintained cross-network bridges connecting the decentralized Diaspora* social ecosystem, preceding the modern Fediverse by years.

4. 2011 – 2015: Linux Server Clusters, Red5 Media Streaming & Consortium News

From 2011 onward, the network operated dedicated bare-metal Linux server clusters running Debian, Ubuntu, and CentOS, powering real-time streaming architectures (Red5 Media Server, RTMP, RTSP, HLS, FFmpeg).

The infrastructure facilitated live cinema broadcasts from Cannes (France), the international initiative africa.dobruja.com covering events from the Nelson Mandela residence in Johannesburg (South Africa), as well as dedicated cultural channels including audiobooks.dobruja.com launched on August 1, 2011. Syndicated reporting operated in direct partnership with leading national and global agencies (bTV, Nova, BNT, TV7, Deutsche Welle, Associated Press, CNN).

5. 2013: Strategic Turnaround & Integration of bestjobs.bg

The career brand bestjobs.bg, originating from the pioneer regional classifieds network of Neogen Group across Southeastern Europe (Romania, Serbia, Moldova), was restructured following regional labor market testing via jobs.dobruja.com.

On August 15, 2013, the infrastructure and brand stewardship of bestjobs.bg were formally consolidated under Mobikom Bulgaria. The platform was repurposed into an autonomous direct recruitment board operating without recruiter commissions, standardized in Euro (€), and enforcing 100% cookie-free applicant privacy.

6. Modern Digital Sovereignty: The 512-Byte Web Doctrine

Today, Mobikom functions as an institutional consortium coordinating independent regional daily journalism (dobrichnews.com), cultural and agrarian preservation (dobruja.com), and direct employment advertising (bestjobs.bg).

Our engineering center advances the 512-byte web architectural doctrine — eliminating third-party script dependencies, executing transient state in RAM, eradicating tracking telemetry, and delivering digital decarbonization. Operational sovereignty is established not through bloated corporate overhead, but through mathematical elegance, privacy by architecture, and systemic autonomy.
