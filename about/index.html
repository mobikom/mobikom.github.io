1992 – 2005: The NMT-450 Cellular Pioneer

In December 1992, Radio Telecommunication Company (RTC) Mobikom was incorporated as a landmark international joint venture between British telecommunications giant Cable & Wireless (49%), the state-owned Bulgarian Telecommunications Company (BTC / БТК, 39%), and Radioelectronic Systems (12%). It became Bulgaria's first commercial mobile network operator.

  • The NMT-450i Standard: Operating on the 450 MHz analog frequency band, Mobikom deployed base stations with massive propagation radii exceeding 30 km, achieving over 95% territorial coverage across Bulgarian highways, mountainous regions, and the Black Sea coastline.
  • Mobifons & Dialing Codes: Reaching over 196,000 active subscribers, the network originally utilized the national dial code 0799 with 5-digit subscriber numbers, transitioning to the iconic 048 dialing prefix with 6-digit subscriber numbering.

Handsets, Paging & Collectible Mobika Phonecards

Analog NMT-450 handsets were hard-programmed in Mobikom service centers by electronic serial number (ESN). Iconic models included the legendary Benefon Sigma / Sigma Gold (the 1994 World Cup mass sensation), the indestructible Benefon Dragon, Benefon Delta & Spica, and the Nokia 440, 450, 540, 640, and 650 series.

Concurrently, Mobikom operated Bulgaria's primary alphanumeric paging network (Motorola Advisor and Motorola Bravo beepers) and thousands of card-operated street payphone booths under the "Mobika" brand (25, 50, 100, 200, and 400 impulse cards), giving rise to the nationwide telecartophily (фонокартия) phonecard collecting movement.

2007 – 2012: The Shift to Software, OStatus & Federated Networks

Following the closure of the analog NMT-450 network on August 5, 2008, the telecommunications paradigm shifted from physical spectrum hardware to distributed software, open web protocols, and decentralized application services. The domain mobikom.bg was acquired in 2011 and organized by Mobikom Bulgaria under the leadership of Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA specifically to expand these next-generation open technologies into the Bulgarian market:

  • 2007 – The moutarde.org Community: Early French-oriented microblogging pioneers and open-web enthusiasts coalesced around distributed status platforms like moutarde.org, exploring real-time message exchange outside corporate walled gardens.
  • Acquisition of mobikom.bg & OStatus Collaboration: The domain mobikom.bg was acquired in 2011 and organized by Mobikom Bulgaria under the leadership of Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA to pioneer open application services. The architecture integrated the pioneering work of Evan Prodromou (founder of StatusNet, identi.ca, and lead author of the OStatus standard), deploying mobikom.bg as an open microblogging node with real-time status streams and localized hashtag indexing.
  • pump.io & Diaspora* Federated Ecosystem: As microblogging evolved toward generalized activity streams, the infrastructure benchmarked Evan Prodromou's next-generation pump.io protocol (the architectural blueprint for the W3C ActivityPub standard) alongside cross-network bridges connecting the decentralized Diaspora* federated social network and early precursors to the modern Fediverse / Mastodon ecosystem.

2011 – 2015: Server Infrastructure, Internet TV, Tourism Media & Broadcast Partnerships

Organized under Mobikom Bulgaria, this era marked the deployment of comprehensive hardware and software server infrastructure supporting the transition of Dobruja Media Group from traditional print publishing to rich interactive Internet Media, live Web TV broadcasting, medical video channels, tourism media, and global broadcast partnerships:

  • Autonomous Linux Server Infrastructure: Deployed and managed proprietary bare-metal and virtualized Linux server clusters (Debian, Ubuntu, CentOS, RedHat) running high-performance Web servers (Nginx, Apache), enterprise Mail servers (Postfix, Dovecot), secure FTP servers (ProFTPD), optimized relational Database engines (MySQL, MariaDB), and caching layers.
  • Red5 Media Streaming & Video Production: Engineered real-time RTMP, RTSP, and HLS streaming pipelines utilizing the open-source Red5 Media Server and FFmpeg transcoding, supporting automated scheduling, mixed TV programming, movies/films, documentary broadcasts, and live camera streams.
  • Academic Medical Television & Health News: Broadcast educational video content and specialized health programming in cooperation with Med TV of Medical University – Varna (mu-varna.bg) alongside clinical updates and health journalism from Medical University – Sofia (mu-sofia.bg).
  • Black Sea Tourism & Destination Media: Extensive resort media coverage and content partnerships promoting premier Bulgarian Black Sea destinations, including Albena (albena.bg) and St. St. Constantine & Helena (stkonstantin.bg), in active journalistic synergy with Ruslan Nikolov (bgtourism.bg).
  • Sports Journalism & Digital Audiobooks: Launch of sport.dobruja.com (August 1, 2011) and sports syndication with sportnews.com, alongside the literary audio streaming platform audiobooks.dobruja.com.
  • New Films & Cinema Broadcasts from Cannes, France: Streamed new film releases, festival cinema broadcasts, and cultural reports from Cannes, France, delivering curated international movie programming across the media network.
  • Dobruja Media Group Milestones: Launch of news.dobruja.com (November 17, 2011), establishment of daily news portal dobrichnews.com (July 15, 2012), and launch of tv.dobruja.com (December 3, 2013).
  • Johannesburg Broadcasts & International TV Channels (2013–2015): Expansion into specialized international streaming platforms: africa.dobruja.com (broadcasting nature channels, South African cultural reports, and historical coverage from Johannesburg including the visit to the residence of Nobel Peace Prize laureate Nelson Mandela), planet.dobruja.com (broadcasting a package of 7 Russian television channels), and mia.dobruja.com (HD channels broadcasting from Miami, Florida).
  • Broadcaster Syndication & Institutional Partnerships: Under the editorial guidance of Honorary Editor-in-Chief Violeta Varbanova, the media group established content sharing and news cooperation with major Bulgarian and international television networks and agencies: bTV (btv.bg), Nova News (novanews.bg), TV7 (tv7.bg), Bulgarian National Television (BNT / БНТ), Deutsche Welle (DW), Associated Press (AP), CNN, leading national news agencies (Actualno.com, Standartnews.com, Focus-news.net, News.bg), and London-based newspapers serving the UK Bulgarian expatriate community.

The Evolution of bestjobs.bg • From Neogen Group to Independent Job Ads Board

The recruitment and employment classifieds arm of our network holds deep roots in the Eastern European digital job market:

  • Origin within the Neogen Group: bestjobs.bg was originally founded as part of the regional digital employment and classifieds network built by the Neogen Group (founded by entrepreneur Călin Fusu), which pioneered online job matching across Eastern Europe alongside sister platforms bestjobs.ro (Romania), bestjobs.rs (Serbia), and bestjobs.md (Moldova).
  • Regional Demand via jobs.dobruja.com: Under Dobruja Media Group, the local classifieds platform jobs.dobruja.com demonstrated an acute, high-volume market demand for direct, unencumbered employment listings and recruitment advertising in Bulgaria.
  • Consolidation & Technical Independence (August 15, 2013 – Present): On August 15, 2013, the operations and infrastructure of bestjobs.bg consolidated under Mobikom Bulgaria and Dobruja Media Group. Today, bestjobs.bg operates as an agile, privacy-first online job ads board—empowering Bulgarian job seekers and employers with direct postings, rapid matching, and zero third-party recruiter overhead.

Present: High-Availability Web Operations

Today, Mobikom functions as an umbrella engineering node managing high-availability digital publishing engines, job advertising boards, and wellness commerce archives:

  • bestjobs.bg: High-efficiency online job ads board for posting and discovering career opportunities in Bulgaria.
  • dobrichnews.com: Verified regional daily newspaper and digital current affairs journal for Northeastern Bulgaria.
