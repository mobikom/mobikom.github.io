About Mobikom

Pioneering wireless connectivity in 1992. Engineering independent digital media and deterministic web operations today.

At Mobikom, our mission is simple: deliver deterministic speed, uncompromising user privacy, and independent regional media reach. From deploying Bulgaria's first commercial wireless cellular network to operating privacy-first digital publishing engines, Mobikom represents long-term technical sovereignty and fiscal autonomy.

The Historical Continuum

1992 • TELECOM PIONEERING

The NMT-450 Mobifon Era

In 1992, RTC Mobikom launched Bulgaria's first commercial wireless cellular network on the analog 450 MHz frequency. Operating rugged Benefon and Nokia mobile terminals, it established wireless telephony across Bulgarian trade routes, municipal centers, and maritime ports.

Read Telecom History Paper →
1995 • PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Paging Networks & Mobika Cards

During the mid-1990s, Mobikom expanded into nationwide alphanumeric paging (beepers) and card-operated public payphones branded as Mobika. Chip-card phone booths became the ubiquitous national communications backbone prior to the mass adoption of GSM.

2011 • DIGITAL MEDIA FOUNDATION

Dobruja Media Group Foundation

Under executive stewardship, 2011 marked the strategic pivot toward independent digital publishing with the launch of Dobruja Media Group. Starting with regional nodes like news.dobruja.com, the network evolved into full municipal coverage via dobrichnews.com and recruitment syndication on bestjobs.bg.

MODERN ERA • DETERMINISTIC WEB

High-Availability Static Operations

Rejecting bloatware, invasive tracking cookies, and fragile databases, Mobikom's digital infrastructure adopted a strict static architecture. Built with clean CSS, semantic HTML5, and distributed edge delivery, our platforms achieve sub-10ms delivery and deterministic security.
