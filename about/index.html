About Mobikom

From Bulgaria's First Cellular Frontier to High-Availability Digital Media Operations.

The name Mobikom is woven into the history of Bulgarian telecommunications. Today, it represents a privately capitalized, high-availability digital network operating leading regional media, recruitment boards, and privacy-engineered open web platforms.

The Historical Continuum

1992 • TELECOM PIONEERING

The NMT-450 Mobifon Era

In 1992, RTC Mobikom launched Bulgaria's first commercial wireless cellular network on the analog 450 MHz frequency. Operating rugged Benefon and Nokia mobile terminals, it established wireless telephony across Bulgarian trade routes, municipal centers, and maritime regions.

Read Telecom History Paper →
1995 • PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Paging Networks & Mobika Cards

During the mid-1990s, Mobikom expanded into nationwide alphanumeric paging (beepers) and card-operated public payphones branded as Mobika. Chip-card phone booths became the ubiquitous national communications backbone prior to the mass adoption of GSM.

2011 • DIGITAL MEDIA FOUNDATION

Dobruja Media Group Foundation

Under executive stewardship, 2011 marked the strategic pivot toward independent digital publishing with the launch of Dobruja Media Group. Starting with regional nodes like news.dobruja.com, the network evolved into full municipal coverage via dobrichnews.com and recruitment syndication on bestjobs.bg.

MODERN ERA • DETERMINISTIC WEB

High-Availability Static Operations

Rejecting bloatware, invasive tracking cookies, and fragile databases, Mobikom's digital infrastructure adopted a strict static architecture. Built with Pure CSS Grid Level 2, semantic HTML5, and distributed edge delivery, our sites achieve sub-10ms latency and deterministic security.
