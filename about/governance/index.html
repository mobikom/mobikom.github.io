Governance Framework & Operating Standards

  • Independent Private Ownership: Managed independently since 2011 with complete editorial and financial autonomy under General Manager Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Board-level commitment to zero-tracking data minimization, volatile RAM execution, and absolute absence of marketing cookies across all network properties.
  • Direct Classifieds Without Intermediaries: Direct candidate-to-employer employment listings on bestjobs.bg operating strictly without recruitment agency commissions or intermediary licensing fees under the Bulgarian Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ).
  • Agnostic B2B Advisory: High-security systems consulting, edge network hardening, and green computing transformations executed under strict non-disclosure. Client infrastructures and threat audits are never publicly disclosed.
