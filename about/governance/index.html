Corporate Governance & Independence Standards

Official governance doctrine of Mobikom Bulgaria: institutional media independence, direct recruitment ethics, and architectural oversight across managed digital properties.

1. Institutional Structure & Operational Oversight

Mobikom Bulgaria operates as an independent consortium for systems engineering and media coordination, organized under the doctrine of functional autonomy. Every operational division within the network maintains strict operational separation between infrastructure stewardship, newsroom editorial boards, and commercial transactions.

Governance is executed autonomously by the Operations Directorate and the Systems Architecture Board. The network rejects venture capital (VC) debt financing, equity dilution, and outside corporate board appointments. This financial sovereignty ensures that technical architectures and media publications remain completely uncompromised by political or commercial agendas.

2. Editorial Charter for Independent Journalism

The regional media publications within the consortium — dobrichnews.com (daily municipal reporting) and dobruja.com (cultural and agrarian review) — operate in strict adherence to journalistic codes of ethics and the following foundational benchmarks:

  • Public-Advocacy Reporting: Newsrooms conduct independent civic and investigative journalism focused on municipal expenditure transparency, environmental compliance, and regional governmental oversight.
  • Total Ban on Undisclosed Commercials: All sponsored analysis, political advertising, or corporate notices are prominently labeled. Editorial content is never subject to advertiser pre-approval.
  • Source Anonymity & Whistleblower Protection: Civic inquiries and whistleblowers are protected via encrypted transit channels without IP address retention.

3. Direct Recruitment Ethics Code (bestjobs.bg)

The career classifieds board bestjobs.bg enforces statutory compliance and equitable employment access:

  • Zero Candidate Fees: Candidates are never charged direct or indirect placement fees, commissions, or subscription costs.
  • Direct Employer Contact: Intermediary recruiter commissions are eliminated, connecting verified employers directly with applicants.
  • Zero Resume Brokering: Candidate profiles are never aggregated or sold to third-party data brokers. Submissions route solely to the chosen employer.
  • Euro (€) Standardized Indicators: Transparent salary brackets eliminate predatory wage practices in regional labor markets.

4. B2B Systems Advisory Framework

The Mobikom engineering center provides infrastructure audits and decentralized architecture transformations under the following benchmarks:

Advisory Operating Principles:

  • ENISA & NIS 2 Alignment: Architectural assessments anchored in Layer 7 transport security and zero-trust isolation.
  • Zero-Cookie Transformations: Eradicating third-party ad-tech dependencies and eliminating statutory GDPR consent liabilities under Article 25.
  • Digital Decarbonization: Verifiable software emissions reductions pursuant to W3C Sustainable Web Guidelines.
