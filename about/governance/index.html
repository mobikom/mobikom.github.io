Mobikom: Governance

Clear Oversight, Editorial Independence, and Fiscal Autonomy.

Mobikom operates under a disciplined corporate framework established in 2011 alongside Dobruja Media Group. Our operating model separates executive stewardship from commercial influence, maintaining deterministic digital infrastructure, verified regional journalism, and strict regulatory compliance.

Core Institutional Pillars

EXECUTIVE STEWARDSHIP

Sole Management Accountability

Under the executive direction of Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA (General Manager), operational oversight is unified, lean, and personally accountable. Decision-making avoids bureaucratic dilution, prioritizing sustained long-term network resilience over short-term speculative growth.

JOURNALISTIC INTEGRITY

Editorial Firewall

A structural barrier divides commercial sponsorships from newsrooms at dobrichnews.com and dobruja.com. Editorial decisions are driven solely by public interest, civic verification, and factual accuracy without advertiser influence.

FISCAL DISCIPLINE

Zero-Debt Capital Structure

All network operations, infrastructure developments, and server systems are 100% privately capitalized and debt-free. We accept zero venture debt and zero speculative external financing, ensuring complete operational autonomy and perpetual business continuity.

PRIVACY AS GOVERNANCE

Technological Sovereignty

User tracking, surveillance analytics, and behavioral profiling are explicitly prohibited by charter across all network nodes. Our zero-cookie, zero-dependency static architecture enforces user privacy by technical design rather than superficial legal disclaimers.

EU Regulatory Standards
