1. Editorial Independence & Media Disclosure

In accordance with Bulgarian media transparency regulations, Mobikom maintains strict legal and operational separation between technical infrastructure management, advertising syndication, and editorial newsrooms.

Reporting published on dobrichnews.com and dobruja.com operates under independent editorial charters, prioritizing local civic accountability, verified public interest matters, and regional cultural preservation.

2. Zero-Cookie Architectural Mandate

The network adheres to a strict zero-cookie, zero-tracker engineering philosophy:

  • No third-party behavioral profiling, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Deterministic static content delivery ensuring equal access regardless of device capability.
  • Full compliance with GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) by architectural design, eliminating intrusive consent banners.
