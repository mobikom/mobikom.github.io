Privacy Policy

Privacy engineered into the architectural foundation of the network.

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

1. Fundamental Architecture: Zero Tracking Cookies

Mobikom operates on an architectural principle of data minimization. This website does not issue, store, or read tracking cookies, persistent identifiers, or local storage tokens on your device.

Because we do not engage in behavioral tracking or profiling, you will not encounter consent banners or intrusive popups on this portal.

2. Ephemeral Server Logs

When you request resources from our network, our web servers temporarily record technical connection data strictly necessary for security, rate-limiting, and network defense:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • HTTP request method, URI, and timestamp.
  • HTTP response status code and transferred byte volume.
  • User-Agent string provided by your browser.

These logs are maintained in an ephemeral memory ring buffer for firewall diagnostics and automated DDoS mitigation. They are never joined with external identity databases or commercial marketing profiles.

3. Direct Communications

If you contact our operations desk via email, we process your email address and message contents solely to address and resolve your direct inquiry. We do not subscribe your address to promotional marketing lists or transfer correspondence records to third-party data brokers.

4. Third-Party Dependencies

This portal serves 100% first-party assets. It does not load remote typefaces from Google Fonts, remote script bundles from public CDNs, or telemetry pixels from social media networks.
