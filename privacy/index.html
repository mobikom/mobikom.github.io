Privacy Statement & Zero-Cookie Policy

Architectural Data Minimization, Ephemeral Volatile Execution, and Absolute Elimination of Third-Party Tracking Telemetry.

Core Principle: Privacy by Structural Architecture

Mobikom operates under a strict doctrine of Architectural Data Minimization. We believe the most effective method of safeguarding personal data is to never collect, harvest, monetize, or store it in the first place. This portal deploys zero persistent cookies, executes zero behavioral profiling, and contains zero third-party marketing tags.

1. Data Controller Identification

The administrative and technical entity responsible for data protection stewardship across the mobikom.bg namespace is:

  • Entity: Mobikom Bulgaria / Institutional Network Directorate
  • Executive Stewardship: Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA — General Manager
  • Registered Location: Sofia, Republic of Bulgaria
  • Official Communications Inbox: info@mobikom.bg
  • Executive Governance Desk: stoyanov@mobikom.bg

2. Zero-Cookie Architecture & Local Storage Eradication

Unlike conventional commercial web portals, mobikom.bg writes nothing to the persistent storage layer of your device:

  • Zero Tracking Cookies: No persistent, marketing, analytical, or functional cookies are dispatched to your browser. You will never encounter a GDPR cookie consent banner on this portal because there are no cookies to consent to.
  • Zero Storage Writes: The portal does not utilize localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or background Service Workers.
  • Ephemeral Volatile RAM Isolation: When accessing our static documents, styling assets, and vector graphics, all code resides exclusively in your device's temporary, volatile system RAM. Closing the browser tab triggers instantaneous memory reclamation by your operating system, leaving zero forensic trace or tracking identifiers on your physical storage drive.

3. Ephemeral Edge Server Access Logs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))

To deliver content across global Anycast networks and defend against automated malicious intrusions, our edge routing infrastructure (proxied via Cloudflare) automatically processes transient technical connection data:

  • Processed Metadata: Anonymized client IP address, operating system user-agent, timestamp of request, and requested endpoint path.
  • Legal Basis: Article 6, Paragraph 1, Item (f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Legitimate Interest. Processing is conducted strictly to detect, mitigate, and block Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) volumetric floods, unauthorized vulnerability scanning, and protocol bypass exploits.
  • Data Retention: Transient edge access logs are automated, isolated from commercial databases, and flushed cyclically. They are never paired with individual identities, sold to data brokers, or utilized for profiling.

4. Direct Inquiries & Confidential Communications

When initiating direct correspondence via email with info@mobikom.bg or stoyanov@mobikom.bg:

  • Voluntary Provision: Your email address, transmission headers, and message content are utilized exclusively to evaluate and respond to your business proposal, media inquiry, or technical request.
  • Strict Discretion: Correspondence records are handled under strict professional confidentiality. Communications are never leased, disclosed, or transferred to third-party marketing entities.

5. First-Party Asset Isolation & Zero External Telemetry

External script dependencies introduce chronic privacy leaks. To guarantee zero data leakage:

  • No External Web Fonts: We do not fetch fonts from Google Fonts or remote type foundries. The platform utilizes native system font stacks pre-installed on your operating system.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No tracking telemetry from Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or programmatic ad exchanges executes on our pages.
  • First-Party Edge Assets: All stylesheets, vector iconography, and documents are served directly from our authenticated domain perimeter under strict Content Security Policy (CSP Level 3) enforcement.

6. Data Subject Rights Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679

Under European data protection legislation, visitors retain full statutory rights regarding any personal data voluntarily submitted via email:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: You may request confirmation of any active correspondence records and demand corrections.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the immediate deletion of historical communication records held within our mailboxes.
  • Supervisory Authority Oversight: If you believe our data processing standards fail to comply with statutory requirements, you retain the legal right to lodge a formal complaint with the national supervisory authority:
    Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД):
    Address: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, Bulgaria • Web: cpdp.bg

Independent network publishing platforms and services operating within the ecosystem—including dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg—operate under unified data minimization architectures, delivering direct user utility without third-party surveillance.
