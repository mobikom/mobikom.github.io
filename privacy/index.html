LEGAL FRAMEWORK • REGULATION (EU) 2016/679 • ARTICLE 25

Privacy Policy & Architectural Data Protection

Official declaration of systemic privacy: Mobikom Bulgaria enforces Data Protection by Design and by Default (GDPR Article 25). Visitor confidentiality is anchored not through deceptive consent overlays, but through the physical impossibility of surveillance.

1. The Zero-Cookie Doctrine

The institutional gateway mobikom.bg and its affiliated network properties operate under a strict Zero-Cookie Architecture.

Our systems do not write state or identifiers to client disk storage. Local storage mechanisms (localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB) are completely unutilized. Consequently, this platform presents zero cookie banner overlays — there is no statutory basis under Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy) or Article 6 of the GDPR, as tracking mechanisms do not exist.

2. Data Minimisation & Ephemeral Execution (GDPR Art. 5 & Art. 25)

Pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (ELI: eli:reg/2016/679/oj) and applicable European data protection statutes, data processing is minimized to mathematical zero:

  • Transient RAM Logs: Inbound IP addresses are processed ephemerally within volatile edge memory (Cloudflare Anycast) strictly for L3/L4 DDoS mitigation. No persistent connection logs are stored on physical drives.
  • Zero Third-Party Telemetry: The platform incorporates zero tracking beacons, zero CRM telemetry scripts, zero Google Analytics widgets, and zero remote font requests.
  • Hardware Isolation: The Permissions-Policy HTTP header hard-blocks browser access to hardware sensors, cameras, microphones, and geolocation APIs.

3. Processing Privacy within Engineering Tools (/tools/)

All diagnostic linters, textbook sanitizers, and validation modules in the /tools/ directory operate under a 100% Client-Side RAM Engine:

Tool Processing Guarantees:

  • Zero Document Uploads: Ingested PDF files, audiobook manuscripts, code fragments, and images are never transmitted to remote servers.
  • Local Memory Execution: Parsing, regex normalization, and OCR operations execute solely within your local device's memory and CPU.
  • Trade Secret Protection: Unpublished manuscripts, proprietary code, and sensitive contracts remain safeguarded under Directive (EU) 2016/943.

4. Institutional Inquiries & Communications

When transmitting inquiries to our verified mailboxes (desk@mobikom.bg or ops@mobikom.bg), your email address and message contents are utilized solely to respond to your specific B2B or technical request. Correspondence is never brokered to marketing aggregators, CRM databases, or automated spam engines.

5. Data Subject Rights & Supervisory Authorities

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, European citizens hold statutory rights to access, rectification, erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), and restriction of processing.

Supervisory Authority for Bulgaria: Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP) Address: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, Bulgaria Website: cpdp.bg
