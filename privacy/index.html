Privacy Policy

Deterministic Privacy Engineered Directly Into the Source Code.

At Mobikom, privacy is not an afterthought or a bureaucratic consent banner—it is an architectural guarantee. Our platforms operate on a zero-tracking principle: we do not monitor, profile, monetize, or harvest personal user data.

GDPR Compliance Notice → Corporate Governance

Core Privacy Standards

TECHNICAL SAFEGUARD

Zero Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses zero cookies (neither session, persistent, nor tracking cookies). We do not store identifiers in browser localStorage or sessionStorage. Because no non-essential data is stored on your device, no cookie consent banner is required under the EU ePrivacy Directive.

EXTERNAL ISOLATION

No Third-Party Analytics or Fonts

We do not embed third-party tracking scripts, social media pixels (e.g. Meta, TikTok), or surveillance analytics (e.g. Google Analytics). All styles are served locally; we do not fetch fonts from external CDNs, eliminating IP leakage to overseas advertising brokers.

EDGE INFRASTRUCTURE

Minimal Ephemeral Server Logs

Requests are delivered via Cloudflare’s distributed Anycast network. Ephemeral transmission metadata (such as client IP addresses) is processed solely for network transport, DDoS mitigation, and server security. This data is not correlated with individual visitor identities or retained for commercial profiling.

COMMUNICATIONS

Voluntary Direct Inquiries

When you contact us via email at office@mobikom.bg, your email address and message contents are used strictly to reply to your inquiry. We never upload your contact information to automated cloud CRMs, marketing mailing lists, or data exchanges.

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Your Rights Under GDPR

Under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), visitors have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data voluntarily transmitted via direct communication. Because we do not log or store browsing behavior, we possess no behavioral profiles linked to individual visitors.

LEGAL GOVERNANCE

Data Controller Identification

The data controller responsible for operations across mobikom.bg is Mobikom Bulgaria, managed by Stoyan Stoyanov, MBA. For formal regulatory notices or privacy verification requests, contact our compliance desk directly at office@mobikom.bg.
