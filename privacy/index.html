Privacy Policy

Zero Tracking, Zero Cookies, and Complete Visitor Anonymity.

The mobikom.bg web portal is engineered from the ground up to respect human privacy. We consider privacy a core infrastructural standard, not an afterthought. Our zero-telemetry static architecture ensures your interaction with our network remains strictly confidential.

Zero-Leak Privacy Audit (Verified Architecture)

  • Cookies: 0 — This site sets, reads, and persists zero cookies (session, tracking, or functional). No cookie consent popups or intrusive banners are needed.
  • Local Storage / Session Storage: 0 bytes — Complete avoidance of HTML5 localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB tracking tokens.
  • Referrer Protection: Enforced — Secured via <meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">. Outbound clicks to network properties (bestjobs.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com) never expose your navigation history.
  • Third-Party Telemetry: 0 — No Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, surveillance trackers, external CDN fonts, or third-party behavioral scripts.

Policy Provisions & Standards

SECTION 01

Scope of Application

This Privacy Policy applies to all public static files, documentation, and directories published under the mobikom.bg domain. It codifies our strict adherence to EU GDPR and ePrivacy regulations through technical elimination of data capture.

SECTION 02

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not operate user registration systems, automated fingerprinting, lead capture forms, comment trackers, or behavioral profiling databases. Visiting our static pages leaves zero personally identifiable traces on our origin infrastructure.

SECTION 03

Edge Proxy & IP Anonymization

Traffic to mobikom.bg is routed through Cloudflare edge nodes strictly for SSL/TLS modern encryption, DDoS mitigation, and global static caching. IP geolocation logging and telemetry headers are scrubbed at the edge.

SECTION 04

Direct Email Inquiries

When you contact us via direct email (office@mobikom.bg), your address and message contents are handled strictly to respond to your specific inquiry. We never sell, share, or syndicate contact records.

Technical Inquiries on Data Protection?

For verified technical security audits or compliance requests, contact our operations desk directly without third-party intermediaries.
