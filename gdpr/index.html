REGULATORY AUDIT • REGULATION (EU) 2016/679

GDPR Compliance & Article 25 Audit

Formal Statutory Declaration of Compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) via Architectural Privacy by Design and Default.

Executive Compliance Summary

This document serves as the formal architectural declaration of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council. Mobikom enforces absolute compliance through Article 25 (Data Protection by Design and by Default). Because our platform is architecturally incapable of setting tracking cookies, executing behavioral profiling, or writing persistent local data, our regulatory exposure is eliminated at the protocol layer.

2. Article 25 Audit: Data Protection by Design and Default

Article 25 mandates implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to implement data-protection principles in an effective manner. Mobikom implements this through complete architectural elimination:

  • Absence of Cookie Gateways (Recital 32): Under Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy Directive) and the GDPR, consent banners are legally mandated only when non-essential, tracking, or marketing cookies are dispatched. Because mobikom.bg uses zero cookies of any classification, no consent mechanism is legally required or technically deployed.
  • Zero Non-Volatile Client Storage: The platform writes zero bytes to persistent browser storage (no localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or background Service Workers).
  • Volatile Memory Sandboxing: User-agent rendering operates strictly within transient anonymous memory pages in system RAM, which are purged immediately upon tab disposal.

3. Transient Edge Processing & The CJEU Breyer Doctrine (Art. 6(1)(f))

Pursuant to the landmark ruling of the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU) in Case C-582/14 (Patrick Breyer v. Bundesrepublik Deutschland), dynamic IP addresses constitute personal data when an operator possesses technical means to identify an individual.

Mobikom processes network layer telemetry strictly in accordance with statutory standards:

  • Legal Ground: Article 6, Paragraph 1, Item (f) GDPR — Legitimate Interest. Processing transient connection metadata is indispensable to maintain cybersecurity, guarantee uptime, and thwart malicious volumetric intrusions.
  • Operational Scope: Edge routing nodes (proxied via Cloudflare) inspect IP addresses in volatile memory buffers strictly to evaluate Layer-7 DDoS patterns, block automated vulnerability scanners, and enforce Rate Limiting.
  • Flushing & Non-Aggregation: Edge logs are flushed on automated cycles. Telemetry data is never combined with customer identities, never matched against behavioral profiles, and never transferred to data brokers.

4. Data Subject Rights Lifecycle (Articles 15–22 GDPR)

Natural persons whose personal data (such as direct email correspondence) is processed by Mobikom retain the full suite of statutory rights:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): You retain the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal correspondence concerning you is being processed, and receive a complete copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): You may request the immediate correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Article 17): You may demand the permanent deletion of correspondence records held within our active inboxes.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18): You may contest data processing and demand temporary suspension of communication handling.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): You retain the absolute right to object to any processing grounded in legitimate interest.

To exercise any statutory right, submit a verified formal request to our communications desk at info@mobikom.bg. All statutory requests receive formal determination within 30 calendar days at zero charge.
