GDPR Compliance Statement

Statutory framework for personal data protection, controller identity, and rights exercise.

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

1. Data Controller Identification

The Data Controller responsible for processing operations related to this web infrastructure is:

  • Entity: Mobikom Bulgaria
  • Representative: Stoyan Stoyanov (General Manager)
  • Official Contact: desk@mobikom.bg / stoyanov@mobikom.bg
  • Jurisdiction: Republic of Bulgaria, European Union

2. Legal Bases for Processing (Article 6 GDPR)

Mobikom processes personal data strictly under valid statutory legal bases:

  • Article 6(1)(f) Legitimate Interests: Ephemeral server connection logs (IP addresses, request headers) processed to defend network endpoints against intrusion, mitigate denial-of-service threats, and ensure technical stability.
  • Article 6(1)(b) Contract / Direct Inquiries: Contact information provided by users who initiate direct correspondence with our operations desk, processed solely to respond to their specific communication.

3. Statutory Rights of Data Subjects (Articles 15–22)

Under European Union law, individuals whose data is processed by our infrastructure hold enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate personal records.
  • Right to Erasure / "To Be Forgotten" (Art. 17): Request deletion of email correspondence records where processing grounds no longer apply.
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18) & Objection (Art. 21): Contest processing operations based on legitimate interests.

Because we do not use tracking cookies or persistent user profiles, we do not store identifying cross-site visitor histories. Requests regarding direct email correspondence can be exercised by contacting our desk at desk@mobikom.bg.

4. Supervisory Authority

If you consider that processing of personal data relating to you infringes Regulation (EU) 2016/679, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the national supervisory authority:

  • Authority: Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД)
  • Address: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, Bulgaria
  • Website: cpdp.bg

Exercise Data Rights
