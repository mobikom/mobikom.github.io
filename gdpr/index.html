DATA PRIVACY • REGULATION (EU) 2016/679

GDPR Compliance

Data minimization by design, zero surveillance, and complete technical anonymity.

In accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation – GDPR), this document sets forth the technical safeguards, architectural boundaries, and data processing standards implemented across mobikom.bg.

Zero-Leak Privacy Audit (Verified Architecture)

  • Cookies: 0 — This site sets, reads, and persists zero cookies (session, tracking, or persistent). In accordance with the EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC), no cookie consent banner is required.
  • Local Storage / Session Storage: 0 bytes — Complete avoidance of HTML5 localStorage, sessionStorage, or client-side IndexedDB tokens.
  • Referrer Protection: Enforced — Secured via <meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">. Clicks to network platforms (bestjobs.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com) never leak user browsing origins.
  • Third-Party Telemetry: 0 — Zero Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, surveillance trackers, external CDN fonts, or behavioral scripts.

Statutory Provisions & Implementation

ARTICLE 5(1)(c)

Data Minimization by Design

Personal data must be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is strictly necessary. mobikom.bg satisfies this statutory standard by serving pre-rendered static files without capturing or storing personal identifying information during standard reading sessions.

ARTICLE 22

Exclusion of Automated Profiling

We do not perform user profiling, behavioral scoring, automated decision-making, or advertising segment categorization. Visitors are treated as anonymous readers, and no telemetry data is syndicated to data brokers.

ARTICLE 32

Edge Security & IP Scrubbing

Edge proxying via Cloudflare provides modern TLS 1.3 encryption and DDoS mitigation. Visitor IP logging and geolocation telemetry headers are stripped at the edge layer, preventing persistent origin network footprints.

ARTICLES 15–22

Exercise of Data Subject Rights

Because no user accounts, dynamic tracking profiles, or databases exist on this portal, there are no stored records to access, rectify, or erase. For formal legal inquiries or enterprise data processing questions, contact our desk at office@mobikom.bg.
