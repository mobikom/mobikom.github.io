GDPR Compliance

Formal Declaration of Technical and Organizational Data Protection Standards.

Mobikom operates under strict adherence to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation). By eliminating client-side tracking, cookies, and behavioral data harvesting, our platforms achieve compliance through technical design.

Statutory Compliance Framework

ARTICLE 25 • SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Data Protection by Design & Default

Our web infrastructure implements data minimization at the architectural level. We do not generate session identifiers, deploy analytics pixels, or embed surveillance cookies. By defaulting to zero data retention, visitor privacy is protected before any transmission occurs.

ARTICLE 5 • PROCESSING PRINCIPLES

Principles of Data Minimization

Processing is restricted to the absolute technical minimum necessary for network routing and DDoS defense. We adhere strictly to purpose limitation, storage limitation, and confidentiality, never repurposing network metadata for commercial advertising.

ARTICLE 6 • LAWFUL BASIS

Lawful Basis for Processing

Ephemeral server log processing is conducted under Article 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interests) solely to ensure network stability, service availability, and cybersecurity. Voluntary email inquiries are processed under Article 6(1)(b) to respond to correspondence.

ARTICLE 22 • AUTOMATION SAFEGUARD

No Automated Profiling or Decision-Making

We do not track, score, or evaluate visitor behavior. No algorithms, automated decision-making engines, or behavioral profiling mechanisms are deployed anywhere across our publishing networks or job advertising boards.

ARTICLES 15–22 • USER RIGHTS

Exercise of Data Subject Rights

Individuals have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any personal data voluntarily transmitted via email. Because our servers do not log personal identities or browsing sessions, we hold no personal profiles to disclose or erase.

ARTICLE 77 • REGULATORY OVERSIGHT

Supervisory Authority

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority: Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД), Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd. 2, 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria (Website: cpdp.bg).

Formal Regulatory & Compliance Inquiries

For official correspondence regarding data protection standards or statutory audits, contact our compliance desk directly.
