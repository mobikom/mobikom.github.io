Sea Stars & Pomorie Lake Lye

Biochemical Mechanics, Transdermal Magnesium Mineralization, and Curative Thalassotherapy of the Bulgarian Black Sea.

Abstract & Balneological Scope

Pomorie lake lye (поморийска езерна луга) represents a unique hyperconcentrated natural mineral solution generated as the end product of natural salt evaporation in the coastal hypersaline lagoons of the Bulgarian Black Sea. Developed and standardized through rigorous scientific research by the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS / БАН), this monograph deconstructs the biochemical matrix of medical lye, models transdermal electrolyte absorption mechanisms, and documents clinical balneotherapy applications across Bulgarian coastal rehabilitation centers.

1. Geological Genesis and Biochemical Profile of Pomorie Lye

Pomorie lake lye is formed through multi-year solar evaporation of Black Sea water within protected hypersaline coastal lagoons. As sodium chloride crystallizes and precipitates, the remaining mother liquor concentrates into a dense, oily mineral brine possessing a specific gravity between 1.25 and 1.35 g/cm³.

The resulting liquid matrix contains an extraordinary concentration of dissolved macro- and micro-elements:

  • Magnesium Dominance (Mg²⋅): Over 300 grams of dissolved salts per liter, predominantly comprised of highly bioavailable magnesium chloride ($MgCl_2$) and magnesium sulfate ($MgSO_4$).
  • Sodium & Potassium Electrolytes: Essential ionic regulators ($NaCl, KCl$) maintaining osmotic equilibrium.
  • Bio-Active Trace Elements: Trace quantities of elemental iodine, bromine, sulfur, calcium, zinc, and selenium.
  • Organic Microalgal Fraction: Enriched with natural biostimulants, carotenoids, and amino acids synthesized by the halo-tolerant microalga Dunaliella salina.

2. Transdermal Pharmacokinetics and Cellular Action

Unlike oral magnesium supplementation—which often triggers gastrointestinal distress and suffers from extensive hepatic first-pass metabolism—the mineral ions in Pomorie lye are absorbed directly through the skin (transdermal delivery):

  • Follicular & Intercellular Penetration: The high osmotic gradient of hypertonic lye facilitates direct mineral diffusion across the stratum corneum, utilizing hair follicles and sebaceous glands as natural transport conduits.
  • Neuromuscular Relaxation: Magnesium ions act as natural calcium channel antagonists at neuromuscular junctions, inhibiting acetylcholine release and alleviating muscular spasms, cramps, and chronic joint tension.
  • Anti-Inflammatory Cascade: Modulates local cytokine expression, reducing joint swelling, accelerating lymphatic drainage, and promoting micro-capillary circulation across periarticular tissues.

3. Clinical Indications in Black Sea Balneotherapy

Decades of clinical balneology across Bulgarian medical resorts (including Albena, St. St. Constantine & Helena, and Pomorie) have established standardized therapeutic protocols utilizing Sea Stars formulations:

  • Musculoskeletal & Rheumatic Disorders: Arthrosis, osteoarthritis, discopathy, radiculitis, and post-traumatic rehabilitation of bones and ligaments.
  • Dermatological Therapeutics: Psoriasis plaques, chronic eczema, acne vulgaris, and superficial wound healing via natural osmotic tissue regeneration.
  • Oral & Periodontal Hygiene: Hypertonic mineral rinses and remineralizing toothpastes formulated with stabilized lye to mitigate gingivitis, reduce gum bleeding, and strengthen periodontal enamel.

4. Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS) Standardization

The extraction and stabilization of natural lake lye into therapeutic cosmetics and medical solutions was pioneered by the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (ИОНХ – БАН) under the brand Sea Stars:

  • Cold Stabilization Protocols: Ensuring active organic microalgae components and dissolved mineral salts remain stable without chemical denaturation or artificial preservatives.
  • Therapeutic Viscosity Control: Formulating specialized mineral massage gels, compress solutions, and curative bath salts that retain optimal dermal contact time.
