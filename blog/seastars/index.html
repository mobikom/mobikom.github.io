Sea Stars & Pomorie Lye: Mineral Balneology & Digital Preservation

Bridging authentic Black Sea mineral chemistry developed with the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences and deterministic digital cataloging.

Published in Insights • Monitored by Mobikom Operations

1. The Unique Mineral Profile of Lake Pomorie

Formed naturally over millennia along Bulgaria's southern Black Sea coast, the hypersaline lagoon of Pomorie produces a rare, magnesium-dominant therapeutic mineral matrix. Unlike open-sea marine waters, stabilized medical lye (Поморийска луга) is a concentrated liquid byproduct of natural salt extraction, boasting an ionic density containing virtually all elements found in the periodic table.

Its predominant compound is magnesium chloride (MgCl₂), supplemented by calcium, bromine, iodine, sulfate ions, and organic biostimulants derived from micro-algae (Dunaliella salina). In dermatological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation, this hyper-osmotic formulation acts as a potent anti-inflammatory agent.

2. Scientific Formulations: The Role of IONH-BAS

The Sea Stars and Solilug lines represent a direct translational scientific effort initiated by the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (IONH-BAS). Rather than relying on synthetic fragrances, these formulations preserve:

  • Physiological Osmotic Integrity: Active mineral salt concentrations supporting deep dermal penetration without cellular dehydration.
  • Thermal Stability: Cold-pressed formulas and stabilization methods protecting natural therapeutic enzymes.
  • Clean Formulation Standards: Complete exclusion of synthetic parabens, microplastics, and heavy polymer thickeners.

3. Digital Archiving & Open Infrastructure

As part of Mobikom’s digital network mandate, the Sea Stars Shop digital catalog is operated under high-efficiency web principles:

  • Deterministic Static Architecture: Technical product documentation and composition analyses load with sub-second response times.
  • Privacy-Engineered Browsing: Zero third-party behavioral trackers or profiling cookies across all wellness inquiries.
  • Archival Continuity: Maintaining long-term records of regional Bulgarian biomedical and thalassotherapeutic advancements.
