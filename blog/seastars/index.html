Sea Stars Shop Monograph: Pomorie Lake Lye & Mineral Salts

Scientific evaluation of the physicochemical matrix, biomineralization pathways, and therapeutic balneological applications of Black Sea mineral salts and Pomorie lake lye developed with the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS).

Abstract & Research Scope

This monograph examines the unique biochemical and therapeutic properties of Pomorie lake lye as an authentic natural byproduct of traditional solar sea-salt crystallization in the Pomorie and Burgas coastal hypersaline lagoons. We evaluate the high concentration of bioavailable magnesium ($Mg^{2+}$), sodium, potassium, calcium, and chlorides, combined with essential trace minerals and microalgae biopolymers. We document the applied contributions of the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS) in stabilizing medicinal and therapeutic formulations within the Sea Stars series.

1. Geological Provenance and Laguna Lye Genesis

Lake Pomorie is a naturally occurring coastal hypersaline lagoon isolated from the Black Sea by a narrow natural sand spit. Over centuries of traditional solar salt harvesting, intense summer solar irradiation and natural surface evaporation concentrate seawater into an extreme hypersaline brine.

Following the fractional precipitation of sodium chloride (table salt), the remaining residual mother liquor concentrates into a dense, oily yellow-brown liquid with an elevated specific gravity ($1.25–1.32\text{ g/cm}^3$) — stabilized Pomorie lake lye. Unlike standard seawater, lye represents an exceptionally dense ionic matrix saturated with trace microelements and organic biopolymer fractions derived from endemic lagoon plankton (notably Dunaliella salina microalgae).

2. Biochemical and Trace Mineral Profile

Quantitative spectrophotometric analyses by the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences corroborate a dense multi-component mineral matrix dominated by bioavailable magnesium:

  • Magnesium Ions ($Mg^{2+}$): Concentrations range between $50–65\text{ g/l}$. Magnesium serves as an essential cofactor in over 300 human enzymatic pathways, modulates neuromuscular excitability, and accelerates transcutaneous cellular regeneration.
  • Calcium Ions ($Ca^{2+}$): Modulates cellular membrane permeability and provides targeted anti-inflammatory mediation within osteoarticular and synovial tissues.
  • Sulfate & Chloride Radicals: Induce powerful osmotic gradients, enhancing peripheral microcirculation and accelerating local lymphatic drainage.
  • Bromine & Iodine Trace Elements: Natural metabolic regulators delivering proven sedative and analgesic effects across central and peripheral nervous systems.
  • Bioactive Organic Fraction: Free amino acids, chlorophyll derivatives, and vitamin precursors that substantially enhance transcutaneous (transdermal) mineral absorption.

3. Physiological and Balneotherapeutic Mechanisms

Transdermal application of Pomorie lake lye via medical compresses, hydrotherapy baths, or mineral hydrogels triggers targeted therapeutic responses:

  • Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation: Proven clinical efficacy in managing osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, tendovaginitis, disc herniations, and post-traumatic tendon/ligament recovery.
  • Peripheral Nervous System Recovery: Decompression and pain reduction in radiculitis, neuritis, and plexitis through rapid resolution of peri-neural edema.
  • Dermatological Therapeutics: Acceleration of skin barrier recovery and re-epithelialization in psoriasis, chronic eczema, and topical dermatoses due to strong natural antiseptic ionic properties.

4. Applied Formulations: The Sea Stars Series by BAS

To address the preservation and clinical deployment of Pomorie's natural balneological heritage, researchers at the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences engineered the scientific Sea Stars formulation matrix. The foundational technological innovation involves cold-stabilizing concentrated lake lye into stable cosmetic gels, mineral compresses, and bath salts without thermal degradation, preserving 100% of the active ionic structure without synthetic chemical additives.

The Sea Stars digital archive and catalog across the Mobikom network documents this ongoing convergence of academic science, environmental preservation, and regional balneological heritage.
