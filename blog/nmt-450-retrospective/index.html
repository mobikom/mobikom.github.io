TELECOM ARCHIVE • 1992 RETROSPECTIVE

The NMT-450 Mobifon & Paging Revolution

Engineering the foundation of commercial wireless telephony in Bulgaria.

1992 FOUNDATION

RTC Mobikom

Formed as a joint venture between the Bulgarian Telecommunications Company (BTC) and Cable & Wireless, Radio Telecommunication Company (RTC) Mobikom launched the first cellular service in the country.

PROPAGATION ADVANTAGE

The 450 MHz Spectrum

Operating on the Nordic Mobile Telephone standard at 450 MHz, a single base station covered up to 30 kilometers, providing reliable mobile signals in mountainous terrains long before GSM-900 arrived.

HARDWARE ICONS

Benefon & Nokia Handsets

Rugged mobile hardware such as the Benefon Dragon, Benefon Spica, and Nokia Talkman became household symbols of business reliability across Sofia and regional economic corridors.

PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Paging & Mobika Cards

The nationwide beeper/pager dispatch network and thousands of orange Mobika phonecard booths brought affordable digital messaging to every Bulgarian municipality.
