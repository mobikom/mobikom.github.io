Sea Stars: Pomorie Lye & Mineral Wellness

Preserving the catalog and clinical science of authentic Black Sea minerals.

ORIGIN

Pomorie Lake Lye (Луга)

Formed through natural hyper-saline evaporation in Pomorie Lake, authentic lye contains high concentrations of magnesium, calcium, sodium, and trace microelements proven to support musculoskeletal recovery.

RESEARCH

Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (BAS)

Formulated by the Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry, Sea Stars products bridge traditional balneology and clinical cosmetic science without artificial stabilizing agents.

DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

seastarsshop.com Catalog

Mobikom maintains the technical archival footprint, product classification data, and educational guides for Sea Stars salts, therapeutic masks, and mineral solutions.

APPLICATION

Balneotherapy & Skincare

Used widely across Bulgarian spa sanatoriums and wellness clinics for treating joint inflammation, skin conditions, and localized muscle fatigue naturally.
