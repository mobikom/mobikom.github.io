Dobruja Media Group

Delivering verified regional news, community records, and independent editorial oversight across North-East Bulgaria.

Published in Insights • Monitored by Mobikom Operations

1. Editorial Lineage & Regional Heritage

Dobruja Media Group represents one of the most established regional journalistic traditions in Northeastern Bulgaria. Rooted in the rich agricultural, economic, and cultural heartland of Dobruja, the organization has consistently championed verified public-interest journalism, municipal transparency, and civic accountability.

Under the longtime editorial leadership and stewardship of Honored Editor-in-Chief V. Varbanova Prep., the publication established strict standards of regional reporting: primary-source verification, protection of public records, and dedicated coverage of municipal and civic affairs across Dobrich and the wider Dobruja basin.

2. The Two Core Platforms: News & Culture

Within the Mobikom technical network, Dobruja Media Group operates through two distinct, specialized digital properties:

  • dobrichnews.com: Real-time regional daily news delivery. Focuses strictly on breaking developments, municipal governance, public interest investigations, and institutional announcements in Dobrich and the surrounding district.
  • dobruja.com: Regional cultural review and historical archive. Dedicated to preserving agricultural traditions, ethnography, local historical monographs, and community memory.

3. Operational Independence & Zero-Tracker Architecture

Unlike commercial media portals that compromise reader privacy with aggressive third-party ad exchanges and behavioral tracking pixels, Dobruja Media Group platforms run on Mobikom’s privacy-first static infrastructure:

  • Zero Behavioral Cookies: Readers access verified regional news without commercial profiling or cross-site telemetry.
  • Open Syndication: Full RSS and Atom feed distribution allows citizens, municipal offices, and researchers to receive news directly without social media algorithmic filtering.
  • High-Availability Static Delivery: Engineered to remain online and responsive under extreme regional traffic surges during municipal emergencies.
