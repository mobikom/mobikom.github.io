Dobruja Media Group Heritage

The continuous evolution of North-East Bulgaria's independent publishing engines.

AUGUST 1, 2011

sport.dobruja.com

The foundation was established with the launch of dedicated sports journalism covering regional football, volleyball, and municipal athletic events across Dobrich and the Dobruja plain.

NOVEMBER 17, 2011

news.dobruja.com

Expanding from athletics to civic affairs, the news portal introduced fast-turnaround municipal reporting, agricultural trends, and local political accountability.

JULY 15, 2012

dobrichnews.com Launch

In response to growing reader demand for real-time reporting, dobrichnews.com debuted as a standalone daily newspaper platform engineered for high-concurrency breaking news.

AUGUST 15, 2013

Consolidation & bestjobs.bg

The properties formally consolidated under Dobruja Media Group, integrating the national employment portal bestjobs.bg with unified technological architecture from Mobikom Bulgaria.
