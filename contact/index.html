Direct Contact & Desk

Direct communications channels for editorial syndication, infrastructure advisory, and institutional governance.

1. Operational Inquiries & Communications

Mobikom maintains direct, authenticated communication channels. To protect visitor privacy and preserve system reliability, this portal does not deploy third-party web forms, Google reCAPTCHA tracking scripts, or marketing CRM trackers.

Please direct all inquiries to the appropriate operational desk below:

  • General Operations & Inquiries:
    Email: desk@mobikom.bg
    Scope: Media coverage, public interest inquiries, and network correspondence.
  • Technical Operations & Web Infrastructure:
    Email: ops@mobikom.bg
    Scope: Server health, static architecture advisory, and security vulnerability reports.
  • Executive Desk & Management:
    Email: stoyanov@mobikom.bg
    Scope: Institutional governance, regulatory filings, and academic collaborations.

2. Network Platforms Direct Contacts

For editorial matters, press releases, or commercial advertising regarding specific properties within the network, contact the newsrooms directly:

  • dobrichnews.com: Regional news desk, press inquiries, and public event notices for North-East Bulgaria.
  • dobruja.com: Cultural preservation archives, historical research submissions, and civic journal articles.
  • bestjobs.bg: Career postings, employer accounts, and vacancy board operations.

3. Privacy & Data Handling Mandate

In alignment with our zero-cookie architecture and EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) compliance:

  • Email communications received are used solely for the direct resolution of your inquiry.
  • We do not sell, rent, or syndicate correspondence data to commercial marketing brokers or third-party mailing lists.
  • Formal institutional disclosures can be reviewed on our Governance Page.
