Cryptographic Email Transit Architecture

The domain @mobikom.bg enforces highest-tier identity assurance and transit encryption, aligned with modern enterprise mail standards:

  • Strict DMARC Policy: Enforced at p=reject, actively preventing unauthorized senders from executing domain spoofing or spear-phishing campaigns.
  • SPF & DKIM: Cryptographically verified DNS signatures ensuring origin authenticity across worldwide mail exchangers.
  • MTA-STS & TLS-RPT: Mandatory TLS encryption during SMTP transit, eliminating STARTTLS-stripping and eavesdropping attacks.
